Hi there - you found my little piece of the internet. I'm Marko, and I'm glad you're here. Twenty-one years ago I watched _V for Vendetta_ and since then V's words have been burned into my memory -- _Words offer the means to meaning, and for those who will listen, the enunciation of truth._ 

I have spent my entire life devouring good writing and am now looking to contribute some of my own. I've never been a strong writer, but writing is like any skill: you get better only by practicing. While I practice I hope to say something worthy of your time. To let you build your mental model of me here are some quick facts:

- I'm interested in computer science, physics, World of Warcraft and self-hosting
- I deeply enjoy Rust and systems programming
- I'm not anti-AI, but I believe humans should read other humans' writing

Currently reading: _Style: Lessons in Clarity and Grace by Joseph M. Williams_.
